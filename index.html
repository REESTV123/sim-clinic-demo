Quellara product card

Fictional demo product. Use only these facts in the call.

  • Adults with chronic insomnia disorder when sleep hygiene and CBT-I have not been enough. Used alongside them, not instead.
  • 10 mg tablet once nightly. Not PBS-listed; about $69 a month privately. PBS submission lodged, no listing date.
  • QUIET-1, 12 weeks, 912 adults aged 18 to 80, versus placebo: total sleep time +41 min vs +18 min; time to fall asleep −24 min vs −11 min; next-day alertness no different from placebo.
  • 12-month extension (410 patients): no withdrawal effects on stopping.
  • Side effects vs placebo: headache 6% vs 4%, daytime drowsiness 5% vs 2%, unusual dreams 3% vs 1%.
  • Cautions: not with alcohol; avoid driving the morning after the first dose until patients know how it affects them; not studied in pregnancy.
  • Report any possible adverse event you hear about to Halcyra within 24 hours.
