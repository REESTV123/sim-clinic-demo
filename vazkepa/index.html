VAZKEPA product card

Use only these facts in the call. Anything else is a Medical Information request (1800 642 865).

Indication

  • Reduces the risk of CV events in adult statin-treated patients at high CV risk with triglycerides ≥1.7 mmol/L and established CV disease, or diabetes and ≥1 other CV risk factor.

Efficacy: REDUCE-IT

  • 8,179 statin-treated patients with established CVD, or diabetes and ≥1 other risk factor; median follow-up 4.9 years.
  • 5-point MACE: 17.2% vs 22.0% placebo. RRR 25%, ARR 4.8%, NNT 21; HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.68–0.83), p<0.001.
  • CV death RRR 20% (HR 0.80, p=0.03); MI RRR 31% (HR 0.69, p<0.001); stroke RRR 28% (HR 0.72, p=0.01).
  • Around 1 in 3 statin (± ezetimibe) patients after MI or unstable angina have another CV event within 7 years (IMPROVE-IT).

Dosing

  • Two capsules twice a day, with or after a meal. Swallow whole; do not break, crush, dissolve or chew.

Safety

  • Generally well tolerated over 4.9 years. More frequent than placebo: bleeding 11.8% vs 9.9%, peripheral oedema 6.5% vs 5.0%, constipation 5.4% vs 3.6%, atrial fibrillation 5.3% vs 3.9%.
  • Serious bleeding 2.7% vs 2.1% (p=0.06). Patients on antithrombotic agents may be at increased risk of bleeding; monitor periodically.
  • Increased risk of AF/flutter requiring hospitalisation, greater with prior AF; monitor and perform ECG when clinically indicated.
  • Contraindicated with hypersensitivity to icosapent ethyl, soya or excipients. Caution with fish or shellfish allergy. Monitor ALT/AST in hepatic impairment.

Compliance

  • Report any possible adverse event you hear about to CSL Seqirus, following company process.
  • No comparative claims against fish oil, fibrates, ezetimibe or PCSK9 inhibitors beyond the approved wording.
PBS eligibility checklist

Authority Required (STREAMLINED). Initiation 15889, continuation 15927. All of:

  • Established ASCVD: coronary artery disease, cerebrovascular or carotid disease, or peripheral arterial disease.
  • Statin: stable dose for at least 12 weeks (or statin contraindicated, or withdrawn for clinically important adverse events).
  • LDL-C 1.0–2.6 mmol/L (or non-HDL-C 1.5–3.5 mmol/L if LDL cannot be measured).
  • Fasting TG 1.7–5.6 mmol/L.
  • In conjunction with diet and exercise.

Diabetes with risk factors but no established ASCVD is within the TGA indication but not the PBS criteria.
